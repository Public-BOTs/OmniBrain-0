THE IDEA

Task-level intelligence,
shared across domains.

OmniBrain-0 brings manipulation and navigation into one learned decision model. It interprets the instruction, tracks progress, selects the next tool, and uses the execution outcome to decide what happens next.

Specialist controllers retain their own physical skills. The shared brain learns how to use them together — including how to revise a plan after a failed grasp, a blocked route, or an incomplete sub-goal.

LEARNING THE BRAIN

From expert interaction
to a compact unified model.

Three training stages connect embodied knowledge,
domain expertise, and cross-domain consolidation.

01

Embodied pretraining

Establish shared spatial, task, and interaction knowledge from embodied question answering and agent data.

02

Domain specialization

Learn manipulation and navigation decisions from verified successes and successful recoveries.

03

Multi-expert consolidation

MOPD brings both experts into one student through supervision on student-generated decision prefixes.

How interaction becomes training data +

Normalize the interaction, check trajectory quality, reconstruct state-grounded reasoning, and validate consistency. Canonical successes and successful recoveries are retained.

How rubrics refine the reminder policy +

A verifier assesses collected trajectories, and the Meta-Agent refines robotics rubrics from execution evidence. Candidate reminders are evaluated through further interaction, and effective guidance is retained.
